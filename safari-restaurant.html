Our Story

Family recipes.
Community heart.

Safari means "neighborhood" in Somali — and that is exactly what we are. A gathering place for the Somali community in Mogadishu, Somaliawhere food brings people together.

Our recipes come from generations of home cooks who understood that food is love. We honor those traditions every day in our kitchen.
