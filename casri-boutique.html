Our Story

Fashion that knows
where it comes from.

Casri Boutique was founded by a Somali woman who grew up in the diaspora and struggled to find clothing that honored both her heritage and her modern lifestyle.

Every piece is thoughtfully designed so you never have to choose between being yourself and being stylish. Custom orders are our specialty — because every woman deserves something made exactly for her.
